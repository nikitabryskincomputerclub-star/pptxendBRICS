Legal Status
Is BRICS an International Organization?
NO

BRICS is not an international organization under international law.

1
No founding treaty (Charter / Treaty)
2
No Charter or Constitution
3
No international legal personality
4
Decisions are political, not legally binding
5
Cooperation based on consensus and summit declarations
BRICS is an informal intergovernmental cooperation mechanism.
